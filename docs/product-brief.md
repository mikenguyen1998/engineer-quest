# Engineer Quest — Product Brief

## Purpose

Engineer Quest is a personal engineering practice system. It helps its creator practise real frontend and full-stack judgment, save evidence of that judgment, and use AI as a reviewer rather than a replacement for thinking.

## Current release

- Passwordless Supabase accounts with cloud-saved evidence and progression.
- Three tracks with 15 curated missions spanning Fresher through Expert.
- Curated missions with a level, rubric, server-awarded XP, prerequisites, self-review, and saved evidence.
- AI-review prompt export; the user chooses their AI tool and pastes feedback manually.

## Explicit non-goals

- No public social network or leaderboard.
- No server-side arbitrary code execution.
- No automated AI-generated curriculum.
- No claim that XP represents professional seniority; evidence and reflection do.

## Success criteria

After 30 days the creator has completed 15 missions, saved five evidence entries worth discussing in interviews, and identified three measurable skill gaps.
